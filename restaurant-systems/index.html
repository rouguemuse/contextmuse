SYSTEM CAPABILITIES

Six ways we resolve operational friction.

From eliminating ordering path drop-offs on your website to analyzing POS telemetry and streamlining catering intake.

01

Menu & Margin Workflows

Structuring menu item configurations, ingredient yield trackers, and margin maps that connect what you charge to what you spend. Eliminating small modifier pricing leaks that quietly compound across thousands of transactions.

02

Labor & Scheduling Analysis

Building scheduling decision guidelines based on actual transaction velocity by daypart. Mapping historic transaction peaks directly to kitchen station prep hours to optimize labor spend without degrading service speed.

03

Service-Flow Improvement

Diagnosing step-by-step physical and digital bottlenecks across kitchen stations and table service lines. Designing intuitive, low-friction handoff systems between kitchen staff, floor servers, and third-party delivery dispatchers.

04

POS Telemetry & Comp/Void Audits

Cross-analyzing historical register transactions, void timestamps, and promo codes against shift labor. Surfacing patterns where line delays trigger service recovery comps or where discount bleed quietly erodes operating margin.

05

Catering Intake & Event Lead Systems

Custom online estimation calculators for large-format catering, banquets, and private dining. Pre-qualifying minimum spends, guest counts, and date availability so managers stop burning hours trading repetitive scoping emails.

06

Multi-Location Reporting & Signal Intelligence

Unified decision queues and cross-unit variance trackers that compress multiple store exports into clear weekly actions. Operators get prioritized operational exceptions without getting buried in raw CSVs.
