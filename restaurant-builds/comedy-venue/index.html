Confidential Comedy Venue

A venue landing page and ticketing conversion flow built to drive stand-up comedy ticket bookings, drink packages, and guest lists.

The Problem

An upscale comedy club venue in a competitive urban market suffered from poor online ticketing conversions. Their third-party ticket booking system was clunky on mobile devices and did not present menus or show details cleanly. The lack of visual show layouts resulted in high exit rates before seat selection and missed opportunities for food/drink pre-sales.

The Build

We built a custom ticketing-oriented marketing website:

  • Dynamic Show Feeds: High-priority calendar cards highlighting headliners, dates, and live ticketing status.
  • Optimized Booking Funnel: A lightweight three-step checkout funnel that captures table/seat tier selections, drink packages, and guest list emails in a single flow.
  • Brand-Forward UI: A sleek dark theme displaying clean imagery of the stage setup and dining area to establish quality positioning.

Designed Effect & Operational Goals

The custom checkout integrations and simplified discovery layouts were engineered to achieve:

  • Mobile-First Ticket Conversion: A lightweight three-step checkout funnel designed to minimize drop-off during mobile seat selection.
  • Pre-Sale Upsell Capture: Structured bottle service, drink package, and VIP add-on prompts during ticket checkout.
  • Clear Show Discovery: High-priority headline cards and automated calendar filtering to guide patrons directly to available showtimes.
