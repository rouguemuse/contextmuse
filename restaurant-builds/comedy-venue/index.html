Comedy Club Ticketing & Discovery Study

A prototype modeling lineup discovery, VIP table reservation paths, and drink minimum checkout workflows.

The Challenge

Comedy venues and performance spaces often rely on generic third-party ticketing widgets that lack table-level customization, drink minimum explanations, or high-converting mobile interfaces. This prototype explores how to present headliner lineups while simplifying checkout.

The Interface Architecture

Key prototype mechanics:

  • Headliner Lineup Feed: Visual show cards displaying showtimes, door times, comedian bios, and ticket availability at a glance.
  • Tiered Table Selection: Simple choice between General Admission and VIP Booth packages with explicit bottle or drink minimum terms.
  • Mobile-First Ticket Cart: Fast checkout without mandatory multi-screen account registration.

Takeaways

Transparent table policies and direct ticket flows reduce checkout abandonment on mobile devices.
