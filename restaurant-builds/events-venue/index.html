Confidential Pub & Live Events Venue

A custom booking engine, event discovery calendar, and marketing site designed for a multi-room historic venue hosting concerts, private dining, and programming.

The Problem

A historic pub and events venue with multiple rooms and stages struggled with event discovery and lead routing. Their concert schedules, private dining packages, and trivia/karaoke night events were spread across various platforms, leading to user confusion. Inquiries for private buyout events—the highest margin revenue source—were captured via basic mailto links, resulting in incomplete scopes and excessive administrative coordination.

The Build

We created a cohesive digital events platform:

  • Dynamic Programming Calendar: A custom live show feed showing ticket availability, artist highlights, and drink package add-ons with automated daily synchronization.
  • Structured Event Intake: An interactive scoping form for private buyout events. It collects venue-room selections, guest counts, and catering requirements, calculating a provisional minimum spend to help event organizers quickly evaluate spaces.
  • Responsive Stage Layout: Digital floor plans displaying room specs, capabilities, and capacity to help event organizers quickly evaluate spaces.

The Result

Centralizing their booking and calendar management allowed the venue to achieve:

  • +35% increase in high-quality private event leads submitted via the structured intake builder.
  • Reduced scoping coordination time for the events manager by over 12 hours per week.
  • Increased advance ticket sales for ticketed weekday program segments.
