Confidential Detroit Pizza Brand

A custom online ordering platform and interactive menu system engineered to redirect high-volume takeout traffic from third-party portals to direct checkouts.

The Problem

A highly successful independent Detroit-style pizzeria was losing significant margins to third-party marketplaces (Grubhub, DoorDash, UberEats). Because their existing website lacked an intuitive, mobile-first ordering flow and visually compelling item customizers, customers defaulted to convenient aggregator apps. The challenge was building a custom interface that offered lower friction than third-party platforms while accommodating complex pizza toppings, size modifiers, and half-and-half customizations.

The Build

We designed and coded a custom single-page ordering application from the ground up:

  • Modular Pizza Builder: A visual builder interface allowing customers to choose bases, halves, toppings, and crust modifications with real-time price updates and low-tap selection states.
  • Low-Friction Checkout: A highly optimized, persistent checkout sidebar requiring only minimal inputs to complete a payment, completely integrated with the POS kitchen display system (KDS).
  • Responsive Grid Menu: A grid layout structure optimized for high-speed scanning and visual prioritization of high-margin modifiers.

The Result

By delivering a fast, direct checkout flow that bypassed third-party aggregator margins, the brand achieved:

  • +22% increase in direct online ordering volume within the first 60 days of deployment.
  • Average Order Value (AOV) increased by 14% due to smart visual modifier prompts.
  • Direct operational control over ticket timing, delivery notifications, and customer data.
