## Critical SEO Architecture Strategy

### 1. Canonical Domain & URL Consistency
- All canonicals must explicitly specify `https://www.contextmuse.com` with a trailing slash (e.g., `https://www.contextmuse.com/services/quote-lead-systems/`).
- Ensure zero root domain (`contextmuse.com`) or HTTP canonical mismatches.

### 2. Search Intent Clustering & Canonical Hierarchy
- **High-Intent Inbound Lead Funnel:** `/services/quote-lead-systems/` is the primary transactional page for quote calculators, contractor lead engines, and intake software.
- **Operations & Systems:** `/custom/` (bespoke operational tools) vs `/systems/` (architecture & engineering standards).
- **Diagnostics & Tools:** `/signal/` (restaurant margin intelligence) vs `/website-system-check/` (site conversion audit).
- **Proof & Case Studies:** `/work/lone-wolf-dumpsters/` and `/case-studies/edr-party-rentals/` link directly back to `/services/quote-lead-systems/` and `/custom/` to pass authority.

### 3. Structured Data Strategy (Authentic Local & Entity SEO)
- **Organization / WebSite:** Austin & Central Texas technical studio with verified service areas, no fabricated street addresses or phone numbers.
- **ProfessionalService / Service:** Distinct service schemas for Custom Software, Intake Systems, and Diagnostics.
- **Article / CreativeWork:** Applied to case studies and technical essays.

### 4. Thin / Utility Route De-indexing
- Utility routes (`/preview/`, `/contact/success/`, `/signal/demo/`, standalone restaurant build prototypes) receive `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow">` to channel all crawl budget and PageRank to commercial pages.
- Exclude these 25 non-indexable routes from `sitemap.xml`.
