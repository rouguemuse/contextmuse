LEAD CAPTURE

What the standard custom intake collects

The standard Quick Launch package includes a clean, custom-tailored intake form designed to collect straightforward business information so you can qualify and respond to inquiries without chasing people down:

01 // CONTACT Name, email address, and phone number
02 // SERVICE Service needed, project type, or request category
03 // LOCATION ZIP code, neighborhood, or service area
04 // TIMING & DETAILS Preferred timeline, project notes, or qualifying answers

Note: The standard package covers this direct lead-collection workflow. Complex multi-step conditional logic, stored databases, automated scheduling engines, and CRM integrations are available as add-ons below.

SCOPE BOUNDARIES

The Simple Scope Rule

To keep things transparent and prevent unexpected surprises, here is how we distinguish standard website work from custom systems:

STANDARD BUILD

Included in Quick Launch

If your website primarily needs to:

  • Show information (services, story, proof, photos, pricing, FAQs)
  • Collect information (contact messages, quote requests, project details)

→ It fits cleanly inside a standard Quick Launch website build.

MODULAR / CUSTOM SCOPE

Requires Custom Quoting

If your website needs to:

  • Process, calculate, or store data (databases, mini CRMs)
  • Automate, schedule, or sync workflows (calendars, webhooks)
  • Sell or manage inventory (ecommerce carts, multi-step portals)

→ Additional modular development is required and quoted upfront.
