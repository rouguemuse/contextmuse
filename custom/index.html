Built

INNcontrol Coils

Commercial service website paired with a custom equipment intake and operational system.

Commercial HVAC and coil refurbishing services deal with hidden, hard-to-access equipment across huge properties, making quoting and assessment fragmented and slow.
Built property-wide interactive scoping tools allowing technicians to map units directly in a responsive visual floorplan, logging defects, unit types, photos, and necessary repairs on-site.
Speeds up estimation, eliminates paper checklists, and creates a standardized visual ledger of required repairs, helping sales teams convert bids into closed deals.

Designed

D2 Auto Detail

Acquisition framework and scheduling funnel prototype built to capture service intent.

Premium detailing services need custom lead acquisition that handles vehicle types and service tiers, while matching lead generation with local ad creation and content publishing.
Built a centralized custom administration portal that hooks directly to the storefront booking funnel, an automated local ad generator, a blog publisher, and a custom D2TV media player playlist database.
Bridges the gap between simple brochure websites and active business operations, bringing lead capture, scheduling, and local media marketing into a single dashboard.

Prototype

EDR Party Rentals

Inventory-aware rental reservation and date-controlled intake system design.

Event rentals have finite, calendar-dependent inventory (one-of-one arches, slide units, custom furniture). Standard ecommerce carts allow double-booking on the same day.
Designed a custom availability check system that matches real-time serial number stock with selected event dates, blocking out products on occupied dates during checkout, with automated English/Spanish localization.
Stops booking clashes, reduces manual scheduling checks, and generates clean warehouse dispatch sheets showing date-verified drop-off orders.
