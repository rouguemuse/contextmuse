Welcome to Context & Muse. By accessing our website, initiating a project, executing a Project Agreement, or engaging our diagnostic and development services, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following operating standards and terms.

Key Scheduling Principle: Your project is not scheduled and work does not begin until the signed Project Agreement & Scope of Work and required deposit are received.

1. Scope of Services & Project Agreements

Context & Muse provides custom digital systems, consulting, and development services, including:

  • Website Design & Development: Custom high-performance business websites, launch sites, and responsive web platforms.
  • Conversion Systems: Lead capture architecture, conversion audits, funnel sprints, and quote-generation interfaces.
  • Workflow & Operational Systems: Custom workflow automation, multi-step pipeline tools, and internal process infrastructure.
  • Intake & Routing Systems: Structured customer intake forms, client triage engines, and automated notification routing.
  • Dashboards & Internal Tools: Operational management interfaces, database management views, and administrative control panels.
  • Consulting & Analysis: Information architecture consulting, process diagnostics, and forensic systems analysis.
  • Custom Digital Products: Specialized software utilities, calculators, decision engines, and standalone web applications.
  • Hospitality & Restaurant Systems: POS data diagnostics, menu and check-discrepancy review tools, and specialized hospitality operational workflows where applicable.

All custom web builds, operational tooling, and bespoke software projects are governed by an approved, written Project Agreement & Scope of Work. The Project Agreement defines:

  • Project Scope: Precise functional requirements, page counts, database structures, integrations, and deliverables.
  • Out of Scope: Explicit exclusions to prevent ambiguity and technical divergence.
  • Pricing & Schedule: Total project fee, deposit amount, and milestone payment trigger points.
  • Client Responsibilities: Required assets, credentials, copy, domain access, and feedback intervals.
  • Delivery & Launch: Staging review, deployment requirements, and post-launch support window.

2. Deposit & Scheduling

A deposit is required to reserve production capacity and schedule work. Once work begins, the deposit becomes non-refundable and is applied toward the project total. No development, design, setup, data modeling, migration, or deployment work will begin until both the signed Project Agreement & Scope of Work and required deposit have been received.

3. Payment Milestones & Billing Schedules

Context & Muse uses structured milestone billing to protect both parties and ensure clear execution:

  • Quote + Lead Systems ($2,500+) & Multi-Market Systems ($4,500+):
    • 50% Deposit: Due upon signing to reserve timeline and commence build.
    • 50% Final Payment: Due upon staging approval, prior to final production deployment and file handoff.
  • Custom Operations Systems ($6,500+):
    • 40% Deposit: Due upon signing to reserve development queue and begin data/architecture modeling.
    • 30% Core Milestone: Due upon client approval of core UI and functional logic.
    • 30% Final Payment: Due upon completed staging testing, prior to live environment deployment.
  • Custom Software & Client Portals (Quoted):
    • 30% Upfront Deposit: Initial sprint & architecture spec.
    • 30% Functional Prototype: Major system milestone & database schema approval.
    • 30% Feature Completion: Final end-to-end integration and staging sign-off.
    • 10% Release: Due immediately prior to production launch and final credentials transfer.

4. Final Payment & Launch Conditions

Deployment Condition: Any remaining project balance must be paid before final deployment, transfer of production files, administrative access, or launch to the client's live environment.

All final testing and client review occur in a secured staging environment. Live domain cutover, DNS pointings, repository transfers, and production administrative credentials will not be released with an outstanding balance.

5. Changes Outside Scope (Scope Creep)

Requests that materially change the approved scope, functionality, page count, integrations, workflows, or deliverables will be treated as additional work. Context & Muse will provide written pricing and timeline adjustments for the requested change before proceeding with implementation.

6. Revision Limits

Two (2) reasonable revision rounds are included for design and content adjustments within the approved scope during the staging phase. New features, new pages, altered operational workflows, or substantial structural redesigns are not revisions and will be quoted separately as scope expansions.

7. Client Delays & Inactivity

Timely delivery requires active collaboration. If required content, copy, credentials, third-party API keys, approvals, or feedback are not provided within ten (10) business days of a request, the project delivery timeline may be adjusted. Projects that remain inactive for thirty (30) consecutive days due to client delay may require rescheduling and a restart fee to re-enter the production queue.
