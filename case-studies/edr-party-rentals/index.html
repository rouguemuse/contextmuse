EDR Party Rentals

Making a complicated rental business feel simple to book.
Client: EDR Party Rentals
Discipline: Systems Architecture • Mobile UX • Inventory Logic
Status: Client Case Study • Production Architecture
The Operational Challenge

The Friction of Manual Triage

Event rental companies deal with finite physical assets and strict date/location constraints. Before customers can place a deposit, they have multiple critical questions.

The existing customer journey risked pushing every one of these questions back into repetitive phone calls, fragmented text messages, and social media DMs—creating friction for customers and operational fatigue for staff.

Questions Customers Needed Answered Before Booking
  • What is available?
  • Is it available on my date?
  • Do you deliver to my location?
  • What does delivery cost?
  • Does my order qualify for free delivery?
  • Which package makes sense?
  • What happens next?
System Implementation

What Changed

We re-engineered the digital storefront into an interactive qualification engine that resolves logistical questions early in the journey.

01 — Package Logic

Package Combinations

Created understandable product/package combinations so customers don't have to build every event from scratch.

02 — Date & Delivery

Early Qualification

Moved date and location validation earlier in the customer journey to verify availability and delivery eligibility upfront.

03 — Mobile-First UX

Mobile Flow

Designed the primary shopping and inquiry experience specifically for customers arriving from phones and social media.

04 — Bilingual Flow

Dual-Language Routing

Structured important customer communication and intake logic for both English and Spanish audiences seamlessly.

05 — Image Cleanup

Standardized Photography

Cleaned, brightened, isolated, and standardized real inventory photography so items are easy to understand and compare.

06 — Smarter Intake

Structured Handoff

Structured customer requests so the business receives actionable, verified event data before the very first conversation.

Fewer Decisions. Better-Prepared Leads. Less Back-and-Forth.

The website does more than display inventory. It resolves common customer questions before they become manual conversations.
