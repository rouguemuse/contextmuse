Interactive Diagnostic Framework

Self Cartography

A topological framework and interactive coordinate canvas mapping boundaries, psychological variables, and translation thresholds.

Coordinate Topology

Mapping the unexplainable.

Self Cartography uses spatial coordinate geometry to represent states of mind, boundary violations, and communication exhaustion. Rather than relying solely on linear text, the framework plots dynamic variables across four distinct psychological quadrants.

Quadrant I

The Sanctuary

Low external demand, high internal coherence. The space where translation is unnecessary.

Quadrant II

The Threshold

Moderate demand with high translation load. Where explanation begins to feel like a toll.

Quadrant III

The Perimeter

High vigilance and boundary policing. Active defense against misinterpretation.

Quadrant IV

The Wilds

Unmapped territory where external consensus breaks down and survival instincts take over.
