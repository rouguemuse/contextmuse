01 // Flagship Project Ecosystem

How to Explain Yourself to Wolves

Allegorical Memoir · Principal Work

The Manuscript

An allegorical memoir about what happens when a person keeps translating herself for people determined to misunderstand her. The text turns emotional translation, identity friction, and hidden personal boundaries into an explorable symbolic vocabulary.

How to Explain Yourself to Wolves Draft MS • Page 104

“You cannot draw a map of a place you are still fleeing. You can only drop coordinates, light a lantern behind you, and hope someone understands the path you had to carve.”

“The wolves are not wrong for hunting. They are simply incompatible with the road you are trying to build.”

02 // Standalone Personal Framework
From Story to Structure

Self Cartography

The manuscript explores what happens when people lack language for their internal maps, while Self Cartography turns similar questions into a practical standalone framework.

Self Cartography makes invisible personal forces visible. It maps values, boundary parameters, emotional triggers, environmental pressures, and behavioral patterns so users can identify where their choices support—or contradict—their intended path.

01

Values Matrix

Establish a prioritized hierarchy of core operational principles before evaluating behavioral choices.

02

Boundaries & Triggers

Isolate external cues and define environmental safety margins to navigate difficult relational fields systematically.

03

Discrepancy Tracking

Track alignment variance over time, identifying where operational compromises create structural friction.

Creative Tool · Focus Intervention

Brain Raccoon

A fast intervention tool for interrupting mental loops, identifying the immediate obstacle, and selecting one useful next action.

INTERVENTION PROCESS
  1. Interrupt loop: Break the pattern of repetitive, unproductive thinking.
  2. Direct action override: Force focus onto the immediate, concrete obstacle.
  3. Restore momentum: Choose exactly one small, useful action to execute immediately.
