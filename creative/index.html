02 // Standalone Personal Framework
From Story to Structure

Self Cartography

The manuscript explores what happens when people lack language for their internal maps, while Self Cartography turns similar questions into a practical standalone framework.

Self Cartography is an interactive reflection framework for identifying the values, boundaries, triggers, and recurring patterns that shape a person’s choices. It helps users see where their behavior supports—or conflicts with—the life they say they want.
