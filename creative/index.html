Featured Writing Project

Allegorical Memoir • Completed Manuscript

How to Explain Yourself to Wolves

"An allegorical memoir about the exhausting work of translating yourself for people determined to misunderstand you—and what happens when explanation is no longer a path to safety."

The project uses an interconnected symbolic landscape of roads, maps, wolves, weather, rooms, lanterns, and cartographers to examine identity, survival, family systems, neurodivergence, coercion, motherhood, memory, and the limits of being understood.

Status: Completed manuscript • Representation inquiries welcome

Information Modeling

Self Cartography

Status: COMPANION FRAMEWORK · DIGITAL EXPERIENCE IN DEVELOPMENT

Self Cartography acts as a companion framework and interactive field-work installation connected to *How to Explain Yourself to Wolves*. Rather than relying on simple text entries, it establishes a structured dictionary catalog mapping boundaries, interpersonal gates, and conceptual weather patterns.

Symbolic Glossary

A relational taxonomy defining symbols like lanterns, cartographers, and mapped roads to ensure narrative continuity.

Logical Boundary Outlines

Defining rules for human interaction gates, describing constraints where self-translation stops yielding safety.

Environmental Variables

Mapping external variables (weather patterns, road coordinates) that influence how communication scales.

Development Note: Interactive visual modules, canvas-based nodes, and digital glossaries are currently in active R&D. No live digital tools are currently available for public registration.
