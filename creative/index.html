Companion Framework · Values Mapping System

Self Cartography

People often operate from a set of core values they have never stopped to identify. Because those values remain invisible, they may not recognize the discrepancies between what they believe, what they tolerate, and how they actually make decisions.

They may also struggle to identify their love languages, triggers, boundaries, environmental pressures, and recurring relational patterns. Without language for those forces, they cannot effectively navigate them.

Self Cartography helps users make those invisible forces visible. It maps values, relational cues, triggers, boundaries, environmental variables, emotional conditions, and recurring patterns so users can identify where their choices support—or contradict—the life they believe they want.

01

Core Values Mapping

Identify the values driving decisions before examining where choices contradict or reinforce them.

02

Trigger & Boundary Fields

Map love languages, triggers, and boundary conditions into a structured vocabulary for navigating relationships.

03

Environmental Variables

Track external pressure, environmental conditions, and recurring patterns that shape decisions invisibly.
