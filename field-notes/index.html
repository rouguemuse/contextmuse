01 / AI in practice

AI tips you can actually use.

Original short-form field notes. Published here as an initial archive; future cadence is not automated yet.

Tip 01 / Better prompting

Give AI a job, a boundary, and a test.

Instead of “make my report better,” specify the intended decision, available evidence, constraints, and acceptance criteria. Ask the model to flag anything it cannot verify.

Use the prompt pattern ↓

Task: Identify the top three operational risks in this report. Inputs: Use only the figures I provided. Rules: Separate observed facts from possible causes. Deliverable: A ranked action table with evidence, confidence, owner, and next check.

Tip 02 / Quality control

Make the model show its evidence.

A confident explanation is not the same as a verified finding. Ask what specific row, source page, or provided quote supports each claim. If there is no source, label it an inference or remove it.

Try the evidence test ↓

“For each finding, quote or identify the supporting source. Mark unsupported points as hypotheses. Do not invent missing totals. Tell me what additional data would confirm the recommendation.”

Tip 03 / Business systems

Analyze the report you already have.

Before buying another dashboard, identify the decisions your existing exports could answer. Define the columns, check missing values, compare like-for-like periods, and prioritize actions by impact and effort.

Try the report review ↓

“Analyze only the attached report. First inspect data quality and coverage. Then identify no more than five anomalies. Rank them by possible business impact, required action, and confidence. Do not present projected savings as facts.”

Privacy note: avoid uploading sensitive customer, employee, health, or financial records to AI tools without appropriate authorization, safeguards, and an approved data-handling agreement.
