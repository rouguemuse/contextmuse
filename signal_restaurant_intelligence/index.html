EXAMPLE DELIVERABLE

Your priorities this month

No generic 40-page consulting binders or dashboard noise. You receive ranked, practical operational moves with clear financial estimates, confidence levels, effort ratings, and data-backed rationale.

PRIORITY #1 • LABOR SCHEDULING

Reduce Monday–Tuesday closing labor

Opportunity: $620–$870 / mo Confidence: High Effort: Low

Hourly guest count drops sharply after 9:15 PM on early-week shifts, yet closing staffing maintains 3 BOH and 2 FOH personnel until 11:30 PM. Adjusting cut times to match actual guest flow reclaims ~18 labor hours weekly with zero impact on speed or closing sanitation.

Supporting Data Source: POS Hourly Sales & Labor Summary (Toast / 7shifts / Square)
PRIORITY #2 • MENU MARGIN ENGINEERING

Reprice four high-volume, low-margin items

Opportunity: $940–$1,300 / mo Confidence: Med–High Effort: Low

Four top-velocity appetizers and sides experienced 11–16% ingredient cost creep over the last quarter, compressing item contribution margin below 58%. A targeted $1.00–$1.50 menu price adjustment restores target margins without impacting order volume.

Supporting Data Source: PMIX Report + Current Menu Pricing + Theoretical Food Cost
PRIORITY #3 • PURCHASING & YIELD

Investigate chicken purchasing variance

Potential Exposure: $410 / mo Confidence: Medium Effort: Medium

Purchased case weight for boneless poultry exceeds recipe theoretical usage by 18.4% across a 60-day billing cycle. Points to either prep trim yield inconsistency or portion weight drift on the grill station.

Supporting Data Source: Vendor Invoices (US Foods / Sysco) vs. Recipe Theoretical Cost
