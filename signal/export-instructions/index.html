Signal Export Instructions (Toast)

For the most accurate analysis, please export reports in CSV format whenever available.

Step 1

Open Toast Reports

Log in to your Toast Web dashboard (toasttab.com). In the main navigation, open the export center:

Navigate to: Reports → Exports
Step 2

Set Date Range

To filter out transient weather/holiday spikes and detect true structural margin leakage:

Recommended Window
Last 90–120 days
Step 3

Export These Reports

Required
  • Product Mix
  • Discounts / Comps / Voids
  • Sales by Hour
  • Labor Summary
Recommended
  • Modifier Sales
  • Hourly Labor
  • Promo Redemptions
  • Revenue Center Sales
  • Guest Counts
Deep Review (Optional)
  • KDS Ticket Times
  • Manager Overrides
  • Refund Reports
  • Open Items / Special Requests
Step 4

Export Format

Flexible Ingestion

All standard export formats accepted: CSV, Excel (.xlsx, .xls), PDF summary reports, and ZIP bundles.

Direct POS exports (CSV or Excel) provide the deepest item-by-item granularity, but PDF summary sheets and payroll exports are also fully accepted. Send whatever reports your system outputs.

Accepted Formats:
✓ CSV Spreadsheets
✓ Excel (.xlsx / .xls)
✓ PDF Summary Reports
✓ ZIP Compressed Folders
Step 5

Upload

Compress all exported files into one ZIP folder and upload directly to Signal.

Filename example: [RestaurantName]_Toast_Exports_90Days.zip

Ready to Audit Your Data?

Upload your compressed export file directly to our intake engine, or test the engine right now in our live browser demo.
