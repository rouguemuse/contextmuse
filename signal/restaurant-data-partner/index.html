ANALYTICAL RIGOR

The difference between raw totals and actionable findings

A report that merely lists totals leaves all the heavy lifting to the operator. Signal checks whether the pattern points toward configuration, training, workflow or individual activity—and identifies what needs to be verified on the floor.

EXAMPLE // PROMOTION ECONOMICS

Discount Structure: Capped Dollar vs. Percentage Concession

Two promotions may sound interchangeable operationally, but their financial impact diverges sharply depending on check sizes:

Structure A (Capped) $5 off orders of $50+
Structure B (Uncapped) 10% off with no cap

On a $120 eligible dinner check, Structure A costs $7 less in direct discount expense than Structure B ($5 vs $12). On large party or catering checks, uncapped percentage discounts can quietly give away dozens of margin dollars per transaction.

Note: Signal does not assume equal customer goodwill, incremental demand, or profitability. True margin analysis requires relevant cost information rather than naive assumptions.

What Signal determines: Identifies which discount setups create unintended margin concessions and highlights specific floor questions to verify before altering your menu or marketing.
More Examples: Modifier Pricing & Station Audits
Modifier Pricing (Targeted Tweaks vs. Wholesale Overhauls): When margins compress, operators often feel pressured to raise base menu prices across the board. In reality, transaction logs often reveal that entree pricing is completely sound—the quiet leakage comes from 4 or 5 high-frequency modifiers (extra protein, dairy substitutions, specialty sides) that are either uncharged in kitchen notes or priced below ingredient cost.
Station Discrepancies (Process vs. Person): If void or discount logs spike at a particular station, the intuitive reaction might be staff discipline. But if multiple staff members display the exact same pattern across different shifts, the issue is almost never bad intent. It is usually an ambiguous POS screen layout, missing split-item buttons, or conflicting shift instructions. Signal flags what to inspect on the floor before taking action.

DATA GOVERNANCE

How your data is handled

We treat operating records with strict professional discretion. Here is exactly what happens with your data:

  • Scope agreed before transfer Submitting this preliminary form only indicates interest. The specific focus, questions, and data-use terms are agreed directly with you before any files are shared.
  • Direct analytical purpose Your records are analyzed solely to deliver your agreed report and to test Signal’s anomaly detection logic against real-world operating edge cases.
  • Private, non-public processing Analysis is conducted by Jayme using proprietary scripts and secure, non-public analytical APIs. Data is never sold, shared, or used to train third-party public AI models.
  • Strict retention & deletion Uploaded source files are kept during active analysis and removed from active storage within 30 days of report delivery per our privacy policy, unless you request otherwise.
  • Generalized knowledge only Reusable knowledge developed for Signal consists of generalized operational rules and calculation logic—never raw customer transactions or identifiable restaurant data.
  • No unauthorized case studies Any potential public reference or case study requires your separate, explicit written consent and complete anonymization beforehand.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Data Partner analysis cost?
Nothing. Selected Data Partners receive an agreed Signal analysis at no charge in exchange for useful real-world operating records and honest feedback on the findings.
What POS or back-office systems do you accept?
Signal works with whatever format your software exports—including Toast, Square, Clover, Aloha, Revel, or custom spreadsheets. CSV, Excel, PDF summary sheets, and ZIP archives are all accepted.
Do I need to give you administrative access to my POS?
No. Standard exported files you download yourself are completely sufficient. POS administrator access is completely unnecessary. If restricted export-only access is used, it is optional, platform-dependent, and agreed in advance. We never ask for passwords.
Can single-location independent restaurants qualify?
Yes. You do not need to be a large multi-unit venue. Tightly run single-unit independent restaurants often yield the clearest analytical insights.
How long does the review take once records are shared?
Typically 5 to 7 business days from the receipt of files and agreed focus area. Jayme will confirm delivery and discuss the timeline directly with you.
