Operator Floor Validation Protocol

Zero-Cost Floor Audits

Before making structural schedule or menu modifications, verify these operational conditions in the physical restaurant:

  1. Validate Overtime Schedule: Pull last 6 weeks of weekly schedules for the KDS role. Verify whether overtime is concentrated on prep days vs. active service shifts.
  2. Audit Void Timestamps: Filter void detail records by hour of day. If voids cluster between 5:45 PM and 7:30 PM, the root cause is line throughput, not cashier error.
  3. Test Promo Blackout: Disable "Spend $50, save 10%" for 7 consecutive days. If total check volume stays within 4% of prior week, the promo was cannibalizing full-price demand.
  4. Kitchen Pass Timing: Station GM at the expeditor station during Friday 6:00 PM peak. Time 10 consecutive tickets. If order-to-window exceeds 19 minutes, throughput constraint is confirmed.

                Toast POS Export Guide
            

            

                For the most accurate analysis, please export reports in CSV format whenever available.
            


            

                

                    Step 1 — Open Toast Reports

                    Navigate to: Reports → Exports
                


                

                    Step 2 — Set Date Range

                    Recommended: Last 90–120 days
                


                

                    Step 3 — Export These Reports

                    

                        Required: Product Mix, Discounts / Comps / Voids, Sales by Hour, Labor Summary

                        Recommended: Modifier Sales, Hourly Labor, Promo Redemptions, Revenue Center Sales, Guest Counts

                        Deep Review: KDS Ticket Times, Manager Overrides, Refund Reports, Open Items
                    

                


                

                    Step 4 — Export Format (IMPORTANT)

                    Please export as: CSV FORMAT ONLY

                    Avoid screenshots, PDFs, printed reports, or manually edited spreadsheets.
                


                

                    Step 5 — Upload

                    Compress all files into one ZIP folder and upload to Signal.
