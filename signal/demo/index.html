How to Check This in Your Restaurant

Simple Floor Checks

Before making schedule or menu changes, verify these conditions in your store:

  1. Check Overtime Shifts: Look at the last 6 weeks of kitchen schedules. See whether overtime happens on morning prep or active dinner service.
  2. Check Void Times: Look at what time voids happen. If they cluster between 6:00 PM and 8:30 PM, it's kitchen speed, not cashier mistakes.
  3. Test Promo Cap: Put a $5 maximum discount on the 10% offer. If orders don't drop, the uncapped discount was giving away margin for free.
  4. Watch the Kitchen Pass: Have the GM stand at the pass on Friday at 6:30 PM for 20 tickets. Watch if order-to-window times blow past 20 minutes.
