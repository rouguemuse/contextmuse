I // SYSTEM BOUNDARIES

What do I give you?

You provide standard operational exports: transaction logs, point-of-sale (POS) spreadsheets, timecard reports, labor rosters, void reports, or scheduling audits covering 30 to 90 days. We accept raw CSV, Excel, or JSON dumps directly from your system.

What does Signal analyze?

We analyze operations across key risk areas: transaction comps/void patterns, throughput bottlenecks, scheduling vs. revenue timing, menu modifier anomalies, and high-frequency discount leaks.

What do I receive?

You receive a clear, plain-language diagnostic audit. It lists prioritized operating issues, estimated financial impact and measurable improvement targets, and a floor-validation script to verify findings before changing active procedures.

How much does it cost?

Signal uses a transparent three-tier offer structure starting with the Signal Snapshot at $149 Founding Rate ($195 standard), scaling to $595 for comprehensive diagnostics, and $195/month for scheduled ongoing reviews.
