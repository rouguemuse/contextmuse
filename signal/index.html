The Six Operational Intersections Signal Examines

POS systems keep your reports in separate silos. Signal pulls them into an integrated model to reveal what is happening between the lines:

1. Sales vs. Labor Timing

Identifies when labor is scheduled against peak guest arrivals versus when the kitchen actually gets backed up.

2. Labor Hours vs. Void Cascades

Detects shift transitions where understaffed floor stations generate elevated mistake voids and comped drinks.

3. Discount Rule vs. Check Size

Uncovers promotional rules where percentage discounts give away more margin than fixed-dollar offers on high-top tables.

4. Ticket Pacing vs. Margin Erosion

Measures table dwell times against kitchen ticket queues to show where kitchen delays suppress profitable turn volume.

5. Product Mix vs. Prep Complexity

Flags complex modifier items that overwhelm cook stations during rushes and cause kitchen-wide slowdowns.

6. Station Load vs. Floor Comps

Correlates station ticket volume with server-entered apology comps to isolate station-specific operational breakdowns.

Floor staff use generic discount keys when buttons lack strict POS validation rules.

$2,340 Verified Loss

OBSERVED EVIDENCE FROM TOAST CSV:
  • 42 checks used open "VIP/Promo" button without required manager authorization code.
  • Comp frequency surges 3.4x between 2:45 PM and 3:30 PM (shift transition window).
  • Station 3 accounts for 68% of all discretionary food voids during Friday dinner service.
Operator Floor Check:

Check POS permissions for open discount keys. Require reason codes on discounts over $10. Review 3 PM shift change drawer counts.

FINDING F-04 // MODIFIER OMISSIONS

Modifier Omission & Add-on Attrition

Kitchen prep slips show billable proteins and premium sides requested via free special-instruction notes.

$1,820 Verified Loss
14.2% modifier drop rate
MODIFIER CORRELATION AUDIT:

When dinner order velocity exceeds 45 tickets/hour, servers switch from tapping nested modifier screens ("+Avocado $2.50", "+Bacon $3.00") to typing "add avo" or "crispy bacon" into free text instructions.

Result: 520 unbilled premium additions across the 90-day test window = $1,820 unrecovered food cost.
Operator POS Fix:

Flatten the modifier tree: move the top 4 add-ons directly to the primary item selection screen to eliminate custom typing friction.

VI // FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions from restaurant operators.

Clear answers about data requirements, turnaround times, and report deliverables.

What data do you need? +
Signal works directly from standard reports your POS and scheduling tools already produce. Typically, that means an Item Sales Summary, a Product Mix (PMIX) report, an Hourly Sales and Labor report, and a Discount or Comp detail file covering 30 to 90 days. You do not need to generate special formats.
Do I need to clean my exports before sending? +
No. Please do not spend time cleaning, formatting, or merging your files. Signal handles raw exports, mismatched column headers, and multi-tab spreadsheets directly. Send whatever your system produces.
Do you need direct access to my POS or administrative passwords? +
No. Signal is built specifically to operate from exported reports. We never ask for administrative credentials, master passwords, or permanent live integrations. If you prefer not to pull reports yourself, you may optionally grant temporary, export-only access to your POS portal.
What file types can I send? +
We accept CSV (.csv), Excel workbooks (.xlsx, .xls), tab-delimited files (.tsv, .txt), and standard tabular PDF reports. Multiple files can be uploaded together via our intake form or emailed directly.
Can you work with Toast, Square, Clover, etc.? +
Yes. Signal works with Toast, Square, Clover, Lightspeed, Revel, Aloha, Micros, NCR, TouchBistro, and any other system that allows tabular data export. See our Toast Export Instructions for step-by-step guidance.
What happens to my data and privacy? +
Your operating data is treated as confidential business information. It is processed securely on isolated machines, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never combined into public benchmarks without explicit written permission. All client data can be deleted upon request once analysis is complete.
How long does the analysis take? +
Turnaround depends on the tier: the Signal Snapshot ($195) is completed in 1–2 business days. The Signal Diagnostic ($595) takes 3–5 business days. The Signal Deep Dive ($1,250+) takes 7–10 business days.
What if my exports are messy, fragmented, or missing dates? +
Messy records are normal in independent restaurant operations. When Signal ingests your files, our parsing scripts identify gaps, reconcile discrepancies, and document any limitations in the report brief before drawing conclusions.
What exactly will I receive in the final deliverable? +
You receive a clear Executive Action Brief (PDF and digital format) ranking all findings by verified dollar impact, complete evidence tables tracing every claim back to your records, an in-store floor validation checklist, and exact POS menu button fix recommendations.
How does upgrading between tiers work? +
If you start with a Signal Snapshot ($195) and decide within 30 days that you want the full Signal Diagnostic ($595), 100% of your $195 payment is credited toward the Diagnostic fee.
