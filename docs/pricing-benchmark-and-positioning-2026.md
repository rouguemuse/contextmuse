# Context & Muse — Competitive Pricing & Positioning Review
Date: November 3, 2026
Status: Recommendations for approval; NOT published or binding prices.
Method: Published asking rates and package descriptions. These do not represent verified invoices or a statistically representative national survey.

## Positioning
Context & Muse is a founder-led **operational systems and creative experience studio**, not an everything-agency.
Lead with outcomes: better-qualified leads, reliable bookings, decisions from existing reports, fewer manual steps. Offer brand documents, story worlds, and marketing content as bounded secondary capabilities. Products and templates live in the Lab rather than custom services.

## Critical business rules
1. Preserve existing $395/$2,500/$6,500/$375-quarter/$600-quarter until changes are approved.
2. Grandfather existing clients subject to agreements; never silently change their plan.
3. An SEO network of forty-plus pages with ZIP filtering is a complex funnel, not a low-cost brochure website.
4. Hosting != maintenance != ongoing development. Separate platform bill, operations, content changes, incident support, and backups.
5. Templates and calculators are low-ticket unless they deliver unusually valuable tested logic or bundling; don't compete solely on graphics.
6. Social media full-service management is high recurring labor. Prefer a narrow design/content package unless staffing supports more.
7. Keep customer ownership of domains and data explicit.
8. Protect the locked lead backend and don't route newsletter subscriptions into the sales leads table.

## Field Notes program
One original, practical AI tip per week as a **production goal** (research, draft, quality review, publish; no background scheduling currently implemented), plus a monthly deeper systems or creative essay. Each tip should include a problem, reusable method/prompt, evidence check, privacy caveat, and real example. Field Notes archive and RSS can be used today. Add email subscriptions only after separate consent, subscriber storage, reliable delivery and unsubscribe are proven.
