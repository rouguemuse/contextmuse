# Context & Muse Product Family Brand System

## 1. Master Brand Geometry
- **Master Geometry**: 2×2 grid within a `22×22` viewBox.
- **Base Tile**: `10×10` units with `rx="1.2"` corner radius.
- **Inter-Tile Gap**: `2` units (`x: 0, 12; y: 0, 12`).
- **Parent Brand**: `context-muse-mark.svg` (Dark slate `#0E1F1B`, Teal `#0F766E`, Mid-teal `#1A9089`, Warm gold `#C5A059`).

## 2. Product Family Marks
Each proprietary product receives a functional geometric variation:
1. **Revision Atlas** (`revision-mark.svg`): Branch-staggered active revision tile for non-destructive manuscript lineage.
2. **Website Audit System** (`audit-mark.svg`): 3 solid tiles + 1 coordinate diagnostic frame marking issue location.
3. **The Opportunity Funnel** (`opportunity-funnel-mark.svg`): Inward converging 4-tile geometry from broad input to qualified opportunity.
4. **Signal** (`signal-mark.svg`): 3 quiet ambient tiles + 1 high-visibility anomaly tile with active attention gap.
5. **GenSort** (`gensort-mark.svg`): 4-quadrant stroked container with top-right classification dot.
6. **Contest Rules Engine** (`contest-system-mark.svg`): 3 conformant evaluated rule tiles + 1 threshold boundary gate.

## 3. Brand Governance & Isolation Rules
- **Proprietary Products**: Use their designated four-square product mark across `/work/`, case-study headers, and product landing pages.
- **Client Builds**: *Lone Wolf Dumpsters*, *INNcontrol Coils*, *EDR Party Rentals*, *D2 Auto Detail* retain their own commercial branding and DO NOT receive C&M product marks.
- **Independent Initiatives**: *Maps With Teeth* retains its independent identity as a public-interest initiative and DOES NOT use the C&M product mark.
