Signal

I // SYSTEM SCOPE

Decision support built for operational reality.

Margin erosion rarely announces itself. A 1% leak in discount misuse, combined with a throughput bottleneck at 6:30 PM, can consume an entire week's profit. Signal catches these early signs of operational stress by separating transaction noise from actual structural patterns.

We process raw POS reports covering 30 to 90 days. We look for discrepancies in:

  • ■ Sales by Hour / Daypart
  • ■ Menu Item Mix & Modifiers
  • ■ Discounts, Comps, and Voids
  • ■ Order Types & Delivery Channels

Plain-language findings, not charts

Signal does not provide another dashboard to ignore. You receive a concise audit containing prioritized findings, calculated financial exposures, and a floor-validation script you can run immediately to confirm the leakage before modifying systems.
