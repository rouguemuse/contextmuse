01 Commercial Service Operations

INNcontrol Coils

A commercial service platform that turns scattered hotel and property needs into a structured assessment, prioritized service plan, and qualified sales opportunity.

  • The Business Problem

    Commercial HVAC and coil refurbishing services deal with hidden, hard-to-access equipment across huge properties, making quoting and assessment fragmented and slow.

  • The System Built

    An interactive, property-wide assessment system that maps complex equipment locations, generates standardized intake details, and produces custom service reports.

  • Operational Value

    Replaces chaotic paper checklists and generic service listings with prioritized recommendations, allowing sales teams to rapidly convert property needs into structured deals.

02 Unified Business Platform

D2 Auto Detail

A conversion-focused auto-detailing platform designed around service discovery, trust, estimates, booking, and customer engagement.

03 Inventory-Aware Storefront

EDR Party Rentals

A visual rental storefront with inventory-aware inquiries, event logistics, bilingual content, and reservation controls.

  • The Business Problem

    Rental companies own highly specific, one-of-one event inventory (individual slides, custom arches, tables). Normal ecommerce shopping carts fail because they allow double-booking on identical date ranges.

  • The System Built

    An inventory availability checker linked to the storefront cart. Customers check inventory by date, and the admin system locks bookings for that product serial number on approved dates.

  • Operational Value

    Eliminates double-booking errors, automates translation across English and Spanish storefront versions, and organizes delivery dispatch schedules based on verified dates.

Approach

How I Build

Every layout, database column, and interface route is built upon a standard diagnostic process to isolate and resolve operational leaks.

01

Observe

Examine transaction paths, directory structures, and logs in their native environments.

02

Structure

Normalize messy database logs, narrative fragments, or assets into clean schemas.

03

Validate

Cross-reference timestamps, hashes, and records to locate anomalies.

04

Prioritize

Filter findings into actionable checklists ranked by operational impact.

05

Translate

Present raw diagnostic findings in clear, plain-language interfaces.

06

Implement

Deploy verification scripts and rules to maintain system order.
