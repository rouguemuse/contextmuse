Case Study 02 · Field Service & Estimation

INNcontrol Coils

Commercial HVAC refurbishing website paired with property-wide unit mapping and repair scoping software.

Business Problem

Commercial HVAC coil refurbishing services deal with hidden, hard-to-access equipment across massive multi-floor properties. Quoting, unit identification, and repair logging were fragmented across paper checklists and scattered photos, stalling bid proposals.

System Built

Engineered an on-site responsive visual floorplan mapper enabling field technicians to plot exact unit locations, tag defect severity, attach diagnostic photos, and generate instant standardized repair ledgers.

Operational Value

Reduced estimation turnaround from days to hours, eliminated manual data entry errors, and gave commercial clients an unassailable visual ledger that converts bids into signed contracts.

Case Study 04 · Inventory & Reservation Flow

EDR Party Rentals

Inventory-aware event rental website with real-time serial number stock reservation and bilingual dispatch routing.

Business Problem

Event rental companies manage finite, serial-numbered physical inventory (custom arches, inflatables, luxury furniture). Standard eCommerce platforms permit double-booking the same item on identical dates, causing catastrophic logistics failures.

System Built

Implemented a custom calendar-aware reservation engine that locks specific serial items against requested delivery dates during checkout, backed by automated English/Spanish localization and dispatch schedule generation.

Designed Operational Effect

Designed to answer key customer questions about inventory packages, delivery tiers, and pricing before booking, producing better-prepared inquiries and an organized operational handoff.
